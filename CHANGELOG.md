# Changelog

## 1.1.3 (2026-10-30)

- Select one / select many fields and the color picker take the well color like every other field.

## 1.1.2 (2026-10-30)

- Fields drawn by APEX web components (auto complete, date picker) and fields in item groups (password, popup LOV) take the well color like every other field.

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-30)

- The APEX Developer Toolbar keeps its own look; the kit no longer turns its buttons into raised neumorphic buttons.
- Single checkboxes (for example "I accept the terms"): checked, the box fills with the accent color like in checkbox groups, and a click no longer leaves a focus ring.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-30)

- Light mode is built on a soft, neutral white (#E3E3E3) instead of a blue-grey; popups are a step lighter (#E9E9E9), fields #DCDCDC, and text, lines and shadows are neutral grey. The accent color is unchanged.
- Fields the browser fills in (saved user names and passwords) keep the soft well instead of the browser's white-blue.
- Interactive Grid and Interactive Report: no vertical lines, a light header with one soft line, and the grid's search is one pressed-in pill.
- Interactive Report highlights stay readable in dark mode.
- Colored badges are crisp (no inner shadow); Contextual Info items have padding; Comments fit inside their region.

## 1.0.11 (2026-10-30)

- Navigation Bar lists in the page body: raised buttons like in the header, and the bar wraps instead of cutting off items in narrow regions.
- Media List with Span Horizontal: the items wrap inside the region instead of running past its edge.

## 1.0.10 (2026-10-30)

- Tabs, Region Display Selector and pill-button radio groups: the selected item's raised shadow fits inside the bar (it was cut off at the top and bottom), and every tab is fully rounded, also in the Pill style.

## 1.0.9 (2026-10-30)

- Tabs Container: one raised card, with the tab bar pressed in at its top and the tab's content padded below it.

## 1.0.8 (2026-10-30)

- Carousel: with previous / next buttons, the slides keep a gutter on each side, so the buttons never cover the slide's content.

## 1.0.7 (2026-10-30)

- Stacked avatars: a smaller overlap without the cut-out crescent, a ring in the page color between them and a soft accent fill, so every avatar and its initials are readable.

## 1.0.6 (2026-10-30)

- Badge List with colors (u-colors): the labels take the tile's contrast color, like the values, so they are readable on dark and light tiles.

## 1.0.5 (2026-10-30)

- Fields: more room on the left and right (0.75rem), also for floating labels and item icons, so text and icons stay clear of the rounded edge.
- Radio groups shown as pill buttons: a segmented control - one pressed-in well with the chosen option raised in the accent color (it was near-black).
- Hot controls everywhere (dialog OK buttons, Cards buttons, button groups) use the accent instead of Universal Theme's near-black.
- Radios and checkboxes: no black focus ring after a click; keyboard focus shows an accent ring.
- List boxes (shuttle, select multiple): rounded rows, the selected row in the accent tint; thin, soft scrollbars in list boxes, text areas and scrolling regions.

## 1.0.4 (2026-10-30)

- Page messages: the close button no longer moves on hover; the message icon has no off-center circle behind it.
- Required fields with floating labels: the required dot sits in the corner, clear of the label when it moves up.

## 1.0.3 (2026-10-30)

- Required fields with floating labels: the required marker is a small red dot inside the rounded corner instead of a square triangle over the field's edge.

## 1.0.2 (2026-10-30)

- Fields in error keep their soft pressed-in look, with a light red tint and a thin soft red edge instead of a hard red ring.

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-30)

- No focus ring after a mouse click or tap (for example on a collapsible region's title, or the side navigation with `neumorphism.js`); keyboard focus keeps its ring.
- Page messages: the close button is a small round raised button inside the message, readable in light and dark mode.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-30)

- First public release: `neumorphism.css` for every Universal Theme component, in light and dark mode, and `neumorphism.js` for the light/dark switch.
